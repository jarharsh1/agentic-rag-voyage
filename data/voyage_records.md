# Voyage & Port-Call Records (sample)

> Illustrative, fictional fleet-operations records for a Bahri-style use case.
> Not real operational data.

## Voyage report — VLCC, voyage 0423
The vessel departed Ras Tanura on 12 March carrying a crude oil cargo bound for
Rotterdam. Average speed on the laden passage was 13.2 knots. Bunkers remaining
on departure were 1,850 metric tonnes of VLSFO.

## Port call — Jeddah Islamic Port
The vessel arrived at Jeddah anchorage at 02:00 on 18 March and tendered notice
of readiness. Berthing was delayed by six hours due to port congestion. The
delay beyond allowed laytime triggered demurrage of approximately USD 150,000
at the charter rate.

## Port circular — Fujairah
Fujairah port authority advises that from 1 April all tankers must submit the
pre-arrival security notification 48 hours in advance. Vessels carrying
dangerous goods must additionally file the IMDG declaration before entry.

## Incident log — engine room
On 20 March an auxiliary engine high-temperature alarm was recorded during the
laden passage. The engineers reduced load and cleaned the cooler; no off-hire
time resulted and the voyage continued on schedule.

## Port call — Rotterdam discharge
The vessel completed discharge at Rotterdam on 28 March. Total discharge time
was 54 hours, within the allowed laytime, so despatch money was earned by the
charterers. No cargo shortage was recorded against the bills of lading.
